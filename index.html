Privacy & service details

This is an unofficial student community project, not an official university announcement channel.

Confirmed subscribers receive only events visible to the shared monitor. No LMS password or private calendar token is requested from students.

Your email, consent version and basic subscription timestamps are held in a private Google Sheet accessible to the organiser and trusted editors. Google processes the mail and verification. Cloudflare processes a security challenge only if displayed.

Each alert has an unsubscribe link. Confirming unsubscribe deletes your subscriber record; a message already being sent may still arrive. Unconfirmed records are normally removed within 48 hours. Provider backups and delivered mail have their own retention.

An anonymous, temporary page-activity count helps the organiser see recent usage. It is not linked to email addresses or student login sessions and expires after five minutes.

Checks and delivery depend on service availability and sender quotas. Always verify deadlines on the LMS.
