Privacy & how this works

Unofficial student-run service, not an official SLTC notice channel. Only the monitor's shared courses are included. No LMS password or LMS access token is requested.

We store your university email, subscription ID, consent version, confirmation time and basic anti-abuse timestamps in a private Google Sheet. The organiser and trusted Google project editors can access it; other students cannot. Google processes the signup and mail. If a CAPTCHA is displayed, Cloudflare also processes the challenge.

Unconfirmed requests are removed by daily cleanup after 24 hours (normally within 48 hours). Confirmed records remain until you unsubscribe or the service closes. Each alert has an unsubscribe link; opening it asks you to press Unsubscribe. This deletes your subscriber row. Google account backups, delivered emails and provider logs have their own retention.

No marketing or public subscriber directory. Alerts may be delayed by sending limits or outages. Contact the organiser to request removal if you cannot use your link.
